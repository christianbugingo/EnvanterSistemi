# Inventory System — Privacy Notice

Version: 1.3.0 · Last updated: 6 November 2026
Developer: COODIC LABS, Rwanda · Contact: tresorbi29@gmail.com · https://coodic.org/

**DRAFT — LAWYER REVIEW REQUIRED before commercial distribution.**

All shop data stays on the local computer. The application makes no network calls, uses no cloud service, online payments, analytics or telemetry, and sends no personal data to COODIC LABS. COODIC LABS receives and collects no personal data through the application.

Local data includes accounts, password/recovery hashes, products, sales, receipts, settings, acceptance records, audit activity and diagnostic logs. Passwords and recovery codes are hashed. Access to the operating-system profile and database files must be controlled by the shop owner.

Backups are made and kept only under the user's control: manually through the app and automatically on the local disk before database migrations. COODIC LABS does not receive them. The user chooses where to keep copies and how long to retain them. Receipt exports, logs and backups should be protected as business records.

No support message or email is sent automatically. If a user independently emails support or shares data, that separate interaction is outside this application's automatic local processing; an appropriate support-data notice must be supplied before requesting such data.

Uninstalling the app does not erase local shop data. The owner controls export, retention and deletion, subject to local rules. Any future feature that sends data requires an updated notice before activation and any consent or other lawful basis required by applicable law.

Counsel must review these statements, the owner's employee/customer-data responsibilities, retention and support handling for intended markets. This notice does not determine the shop owner's legal duties.
